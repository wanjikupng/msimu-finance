How it works

  1. Apply.
    Choose a phone, a deposit and a daily plan. You get an exact quote before anything is signed.
  2. Sign.
    E-sign your contract on your phone and confirm you understand the lock rule.
  3. Get approved.
    A loan officer reviews your application, usually the same day.
  4. Pay daily.
    Pay any amount any day. Every shilling counts toward owning your phone.

Why it is different

Transparent total cost

You see the full price, the deposit, the financed amount and the day you will own it — before you sign. No interest is added on top.

Pay any amount, any day

Had a good day? Pay extra and finish early. Had a slow day? Pay less — partial payments still count, never discarded.

See exactly when you will own it

Your phone screen fills as you pay, with a live percentage, balance and estimated finish date. No statements to decode.

Built on Tracom payment rails

M-Simu Finance runs on payment infrastructure from Tracom Services Limited, a Kenyan payments company.

  • Tracom: alternative banking channels

    Pay without a smartphone app

    Dial *456# from any phone to check your balance, pay, or see your next due date over USSD.

  • Tracom: e-signing

    Sign contracts anywhere

    Contracts are signed electronically on your own device — no branch visit, no paper.

  • Tracom: agency banking

    Agents where you are

    Local agents register customers, hand over phones and accept payments on the platform's behalf.

  • Tracom: terminal management

    Every device tracked

    Each financed phone is tracked by IMEI and serial number in a fleet-style inventory, like payment terminals.

  • Tracom: reporting

    Collections you can audit

    Every payment posts as a traceable transaction with a receipt — collections, arrears and risk, all reportable.
