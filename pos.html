How agent collections work

No cash changes hands. The agent finds the customer and sends an M-PESA payment request to their phone. The customer checks the amount and approves it with their own M-PESA PIN. The money goes straight to the business's M-PESA account, and the terminal prints a receipt with the phone's new unlock code.

Keys: number keys, OK to confirm, ⌫ to delete, ✕ to go back. Your keyboard works too.
